Reproduced the Vortex 2.4.2 startup flash on a real Subnautica 2 profile and traced it to the unhidden cmd.exe/fsutil administrator check and dotnetprobe.exe, each allocating its own conhost.exe.
Added an automatic, no-administrator cold-start guard that suspends Vortex until a scoped watcher is ready, then hides only console windows owned by descendants of that Vortex process during startup.
Included the terminal-flash repair script in both the release ZIP and the installed plugin as an optional secondary repair after a Vortex update.
